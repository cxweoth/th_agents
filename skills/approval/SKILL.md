---
name: approval
description: An answer is not an approval, and approval is for content rather than intent.
---

**An answer is not an approval.** Choosing among options sets a direction; it does
not authorise writing to a file, committing, pushing, or sending. If you cannot
quote the words that granted approval for this artifact, you do not have it. Ask.

**Approval is for content, not intent.** An approved intent authorises a draft
shown in the conversation. Anything permanent (a file, a commit, a calendar entry,
a letter to another agent) waits until the user has seen the thing itself. Once
they have seen it and said go, carrying it out needs no further approval.

Done when you can quote the approval and name where the user saw the content.
